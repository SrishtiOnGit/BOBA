BOBA

A little history. A lot of bubbles.

Boba, also known as bubble tea, is a Taiwanese drink that combines tea with milk or fruit flavors and chewy toppings. It became popular in Taiwan during the 1980s and has since spread around the world. From classic milk tea with tapioca pearls to refreshing fruit teas and popping boba, there are countless combinations to explore. Can read more at Wikipedia

- From Taiwan to the World. -

Bubble tea began in Taiwan and gradually travelled beyond its origins, becoming popular across Asia and eventually around the world. Its popularity comes partly from how customizable it is—people can choose different teas, flavours, sweetness levels, ice and toppings to create their own combination.
